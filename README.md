# Find an Untapped Channel

**FlyRank AI Marketing - Week 2 assignment**

This repository contains a ranked shortlist of acquisition channels FlyRank can test beyond Meta ads, cold email, and LinkedIn. The complete report is available in [flyrank_untapped_channel_shortlist.docx](./flyrank_untapped_channel_shortlist.docx).

## Recommendation

**Bet first on Online Geniuses.** It provides direct access to marketers, CMOs, consultants, and agency owners; includes dedicated SEO and ecommerce channels; has low entry cost; and offers an approved path for promotion. Pair it with a value-first Shopify Community test to learn merchant language and recruit opt-in visibility teardowns.

## Ranked shortlist

| Rank | Channel | Why it fits | Effort / cost | Cheap test |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Online Geniuses Slack | 53,000+ vetted marketers, CMOs, consultants and agency owners; SEO and ecommerce channels | Low-medium; $10/month; 6-8 hours | Answer five threads, then place one moderator-approved teardown offer. Target three qualified replies. |
| 2 | Shopify Community | Active merchants discussing SEO, AI, store growth, conversion and Shopify Plus | Medium; $0 media; 6-8 hours | Answer ten recent questions without pitching and recruit three opt-in stores for a free visibility teardown. |
| 3 | Indie Hackers | Founders actively discussing AI content, GEO, citations and distribution | Low; $0 media; 4-6 hours | Publish one transparent FlyRank experiment with baseline, method, results and failures. |
| 4 | DTC Daily newsletter | Concentrated ecommerce audience; 84.2% founders/CEOs/directors and 73% Shopify stores | Low internal effort; paid quote required | Price one secondary slot or co-branded AI visibility benchmark with a tagged landing page. |
| 5 | eCommerceFuel partnership | Vetted 7- and 8-figure owners with strong SaaS and agency buying intent | High access friction; partnership quote required | Pitch the operator a member-only benchmark or no-pitch office hour; do not solicit members directly. |

## 30-day test sequence

1. **Days 1-3:** Create one tagged landing page, a short teardown form, and a lead-source field.
2. **Days 4-10:** Join Online Geniuses, participate first, then run one approved teardown offer.
3. **Days 4-14:** Answer ten recent Shopify Community questions and recruit three opt-in teardowns.
4. **Days 11-18:** Turn the strongest result into one transparent Indie Hackers case study.
5. **Days 19-30:** Scale the winning organic message; request paid placement quotes only after it earns replies.

## Measurement

Primary metric: **qualified conversations** with founders, marketing leads, ecommerce operators, or agency owners who have a real visibility/content problem.

Scale a channel only if it produces at least three qualified conversations in 30 days or beats FlyRank's current paid-channel cost per qualified lead.

## Guardrails

- Lead with useful answers and original evidence; no mass posting, scraping, or unsolicited DMs.
- Use one UTM per channel and ask every lead how they found FlyRank.
- Publish benchmark methodology clearly.
- Test one paid placement before buying a bundle.
